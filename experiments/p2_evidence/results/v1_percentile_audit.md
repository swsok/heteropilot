> **Answer: none of them.** `planner/optimizer/feasibility.py:67` judges
> `p99_tpot × (1 + m)` against the SLO, and **0 of the 9 committed RNGD-CARD
> points has a p99 on either side.** Five pair a simulated p50 against a measured

## 1. Correction to V0

**V0 said c76 was "the only point whose two sides use different aggregations".
That is wrong**, and this audit is what found it. Three points do:

| what V0 said | what is true |
| --- | --- |
| c76 alone pairs sim p50 against measured mean | **c14.832, c25.181 and c76** all do |
| the other eight pair p50 against p50 | **five** do; one (c16.6) is a bucket mean fit |

The cause is in the performance envelope, not in the domain. **The
`tpot_p50` field of `profiles/envelopes/RNGD-CARD/…/tp1.yaml` does not hold a
p50 for every point.** For the five low-load points it does — each is the mean of
two repeats' p50, and every one reproduces to four decimals from
`outputs/rngd_envelope_lowload/point_c*.json`. For the four points measured
2026-08-31 it holds the **mean**:

| envelope conc | stored `tpot_p50` | raw mean | raw p50 | raw p99 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 15.3 | 25.71 | **25.7147** | 25.4357 | 29.3930 |
| 29.3 | 31.18 | **31.1754** | 31.8615 | 35.5152 |
| 59.2 | 44.54 | **44.5418** | 45.1225 | 47.9586 |
| 107.2 | 67.88 | **67.8762** | 70.3958 | 78.1807 |

`lowload_sim_error.py` then reads that field (`compared_metric: "tpot_p50"`) and
compares a genuine simulated p50 against it. So the mislabelling propagates into
the accuracy domain wherever a point's measured side is one of those four rows.
V0's *numbers* for c76 stand; only its claim of uniqueness does not. Recorded as
**D101**, and V0's result file and D100 are annotated.

## 3. What changes on the p99 basis, and why it matters

**Two points change sign.** The margin is one-sided by construction — only a
negative `e` earns one — so a sign change is not a small numeric shift, it is the
difference between charging nothing and charging a margin:

| conc | committed `e` | p99 `e` | committed margin | p99 margin |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 14.832 | **+3.26** (pessimistic) | **−6.90** (optimistic) | 0.00 % | **7.41 %** |
| 15.212 | **+2.47** (pessimistic) | **−3.11** (optimistic) | 0.00 % | **3.21 %** |

The committed domain's note says the error "cross[es] zero between 15.212 and
16.6 — so the one-sided margin charges nothing below about 16 and grows above
it." **On the p99 basis the crossing is between 8.211 and 14.832**, and the
domain charges a margin from about concurrency 9 upward. That is roughly a
halving of the concurrency at which the invention starts to act, on the same
measurements.

**The magnitudes move in both directions elsewhere.** c2.194 goes +11.00 → +3.09
and c8.211 goes +7.33 → +0.61 (both stay pessimistic, so no margin either way);
c25.181 goes −3.28 → −12.00, nearly quadrupling its margin. Only c76 barely
moves, −18.00 → −17.31, because at that load the distribution is wide enough that
its mean and its p99 are far apart on both sides at once.

**Why p99 is the basis a feasibility claim needs.** The judgement is
`robust_tpot = p99_tpot × (1 + m)` compared against the TPOT SLO. A margin fitted
on p50 answers "by how much is the simulator's *median* optimistic"; multiplying
a p99 by it assumes the two statistics are optimistic by the same factor. The
table above is the measurement of that assumption, and it does not hold: at
c14.832 the simulator is 3.3 % *pessimistic* on p50 and 6.9 % *optimistic* on
p99, because its TPOT distribution is far narrower than the hardware's.

## 4. What was NOT done

`profiles/calibration/rngd_card_edf.yaml` and
`profiles/envelopes/RNGD-CARD/…/tp1.yaml` are **unchanged**. Rule A3 forbids
rewriting a measured artifact, and the user's instruction for this audit was
explicit: record a mixed basis in the table, do not change values. Acting on it
means re-fitting the domain on p99, which invalidates the E-A1 corpus,
`outputs/e6*/` and the pinned values in `tests/test_margin_policy.py` and
`tests/test_accuracy_domain.py` — the same blast radius D100 §2.5 describes. It
needs its own work order.

**One caveat on the p99 column itself.** At c14.832 and c25.181 the measured side
has n = 128, so a p99 rests on the top ~1.3 requests. The five low-load points
pool 600 and c76 pools 556, which are sound; those two are the weakest rows in
the table and a re-fit should re-measure them rather than adopt these values.
